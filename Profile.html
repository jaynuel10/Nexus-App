A brief discussion on Open Hyperdocument System

I got introduced to Hyperdocument systems two years ago by a friend of mine, Anesi.

We had both been raving about Notion's system as well as a new product which had just gone live the product was (it's since shutdown) by an engineer name Sam Rockwell

we were raving because we wanted to be the kind of people building and experimenting to our heart's desire. if only.. but wait . we didn't need to dream, we could just do it. And so we did

We started by researching Douglas Engelbart, a pioneer of the OHS system and soon we were looking up Seymour Papert, Alan Kay, Montesorri and Bret Victor. The most exhilirating time of my life. Head buried in a book, lost in thought, not sure how to get out of my mind hole. Did i want to get out? The research got shelved and we moved on
